Upcoming Health Seminar

Topic: Acupuncture and Chinese Medicine, complementary approaches for your health and wellness
Speaker: Dr. Jason Wang, Dr. TCM, R. Ac.
Location: #406-4885 Kingsway, Burnaby (Kingsway and Nelson intersection)
Date: Oct 15, 2015
Duration: 5 - 6 pm
Admission: FREE

Chances are you have heard of Acupuncture and Chinese Medicine. Maybe some of the people you know have already told you it helped them with their health condition. But you may still be wondering what Acupuncture and Chinese Medicine really are. How do they work? What are they good for? Why do they matter to me?

Dr. Jason Wang will give an easy-to-understand free educational seminar to explain what this medicine is and how you can integrate it into your treatment regimen for simple problems like shoulder pain to chronic issues like digestive conditions. The aim is to have some of the most important questions that attendees and patients often ask answered so they gain a good rudimentary understanding of this effective system of healing.

Acupuncture and Chinese Medicine are quickly becoming the mainstream when it comes to alternative and complementary medicine for many ailments of our modern day. This form of medicine is often at the forefront of contemporary clinical research. Don't miss out on this opportunity to learn what it can do for you and those you love and care about. Seating is limited so register early!

There will be a Question period at the end of the presentation for attendees to raise their questions as well as a special gift.

herbal cooking instructions

1. open up bag and dump into stainless steel pot(medium size)

***1.5***: 

2. add 1.5 liters water to pot
3. put pot on stove and turn heat to max to wait for boil
4. after boil turn heat down to low heat(electric: 2.5/10, gas:low setting)
5. close lid and set timer to 1 hour
6. after simmer finish close heat
7. liquid inside pot is herbal tea will supply around 3-5 days per pot(bag)
8. take 2 servings per day WARM(reheat on stove only, no microwaving thank you), each serving around half cup
9. after no tea left inside pot, add water again and recook again
following previous steps---if 1st cooking not enough for at least 3 days then add more water when recooking by around 500ml extra
10. unfinished pot need to put in fridge for storage
11. space away herbal tea from medications, food, supplements, coffee,
other teas by at least 1.5 - 2 hours

12. following these instructions herbs should last around a week(1st
and 2nd cook combined)

***does not matter before or after meal although after meal is preferred

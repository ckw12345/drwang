DIET Recommendation(based on Chinese Medicine)

The following list is what I frequently give to patients to take home to follow or implement in their diet. Suits majority of people who have come to see me as most people suffer from problems created by eating the opposite of things in this list.


No raw food(salad, sushi, smoothies, etc)
No cold food
No uncooked food
No cold water
No room temperature water
*water must be at least lukewarm


Cut down meat(include fish and seafood)
Cut down dairy(include yogurt)
Cut down garlic+onions and sub with ginger


No sugar or sweets
No alcohol
No deep fried and hot spicy foods(ginger not included, unless if you eat very big amounts then would be good to likely cut back as well)

Avoid snacks


Only eat fruits in later afternoon, fruit must be left in room temperature for at least 24 hours before consumption

Only eat cooked warm meals for breakfast

Cut down wheat and avoid white flour

Cut down or eliminate coofee and green tea. Stick with fermented teas(black tea, red tea etc)

What else is left to eat?

Eat more cooked vegetables, cooked mixed rices/grains and plant proteins. also do not overeat.
